## The findings themselves, without numbers

- The capsule head is consistently **worse calibrated** than the MLP + K−1
  sigmoid baseline at comparable accuracy. This is the central negative result
  and it survived every re-run.
- The heads are **under-confident, not over-confident**: accuracy exceeds mean
  confidence in every equal-mass bin of all nine cells, and the fitted
  temperature is below one. The submitted paper had this backwards.
- **Prediction margin and max-probability are near-equivalent selectors**, so
  the capsule margin is a valid but not uniquely strong selective-prediction
  signal. The "capsule-native UQ is a free strong signal" framing does not hold.
- **LoRA raises accuracy but does not fix calibration** — the known
  fine-tuning pathology.
- Most of the miscalibration is the **Sabour margin loss**, but not all of it:
  at matched loss the capsule head is still worse calibrated than the MLP.
- The **antitonic projection** roughly halves ECE everywhere, with no held-out
  split and no fitted parameter.
- Conformal prediction is the uncertainty layer that holds up, and only with a
  per-class quantile — see `results/conformal_table.md` and
  `results/ordinal_conformal_table.md`.
